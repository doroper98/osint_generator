114. The Africans were armed in the Roman 
fashion, Hannibal having equipped them with the 
choicest of the arms captured in the previous battles. 
The shields of the Spaniards and Celts were very 
similar, but their swords were entirely different, 
those of the Spaniards thrusting with as deadly 
effect as they cut, but the Gaulish sword being only 
able to slash and requiring a long sweep to do so.
